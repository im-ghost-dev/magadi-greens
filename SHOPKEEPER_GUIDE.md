# 🌿 MAGADI GREENS — Shopkeeper Admin Guide

Welcome to the **MAGADI GREENS** management guide! This 1-page reference explains how to update daily vegetable & fruit prices, mark stock, change today's specials, and process WhatsApp customer orders right from your phone or shop computer.

---

## 1. Logging Into the Admin Panel

1. Open your shop website (e.g. `https://your-shop.vercel.app`).
2. Scroll to the very bottom of the page and tap **"Shopkeeper Admin Login"** (or tap the small key/lock icon in the top header).
3. Enter your **Admin Password** and tap **Sign In**.
4. You are now in the Admin Dashboard! You will see three tabs at the top:
   * **Catalogue** (All products, prices, and stock)
   * **Today's Special** (Highlighted deals on the homepage)
   * **Shop Info** (Phone number, WhatsApp, address, and timings)

---

## 2. Managing Products & Prices (Daily Routine)

### Updating a Price
1. In the **Catalogue** tab, search for the item (e.g., "Tomato" or PLU "1").
2. Tap the price box labeled **"Price / kg"** (or unit) and type today's market rate.
3. If you want to show a strikethrough discount, enter the **MRP** (Maximum Retail Price). The green discount badge (e.g. *Save 15%*) calculates automatically!
4. Notice the floating green bar at the bottom saying **"1 unsaved change"**.
5. Tap **Save changes** when done.

### Marking Stock (In Stock / Out of Stock)
* **Single item:** Toggle the switch next to any item to immediately mark it in stock or sold out.
* **Bulk Morning Open:** Tap **"All In Stock"** at the top of the Catalogue to mark all items available for the day with one tap.
* **Bulk Night Close:** Tap **"All Out of Stock"** when the market shuts for the evening.

### Uploading or Changing a Photo
1. On any product card, tap the camera icon or product image.
2. Choose **Take Photo** (camera) or select from your gallery.
3. The app automatically shrinks and optimizes the photo into fast-loading WebP format.

### Adding a New Item
1. Tap the green **+ Add Item** button in the top right.
2. Enter the PLU number, Item Name, Kannada name (optional), Category, Unit (`kg`, `pc`, `bunch`, `pkt`), and Price.
3. Tap **Create Product**.

---

## 3. Setting Up "Today's Special" Deals

1. Switch to the **Today's Special** tab.
2. You can feature up to **6 items** that appear in the large swipeable banner on the storefront.
3. For each slot, you can:
   * Set a catchy badge like **"Fresh today"**, **"Direct from farm"**, or **"Special deal"**.
   * Reorder items using the **↑** and **↓** arrows to put your best seller first.
   * Add a high-resolution photo.
4. If you have no specials for the day, leave it empty — the banner will automatically hide itself on the storefront without leaving blank space!

---

## 4. Updating Shop Info & WhatsApp Order Destination

1. Switch to the **Shop Info** tab.
2. Here you can edit:
   * **WhatsApp Number:** All customer orders from the cart drawer will be sent directly to this WhatsApp number. (Enter digits with country code `91`, e.g. `919148112233`).
   * **Calling Phone Number:** The public customer contact number.
   * **Opening Hours:** E.g., `Daily 6:30 AM – 9:30 PM`.
   * **Shop Address & Google Maps Link:** Shown at the bottom of the storefront for customers doing self-pickup.
3. A live preview on the right shows exactly how customers see your store info.
4. Tap **Save changes**.

---

## 5. How Customer WhatsApp Orders Work

1. Customers add items to their cart on the mobile storefront:
   * Vegetables & fruits sold by kg step in **0.25 kg** intervals (250g, 500g, 750g, 1 kg, etc.).
   * Items sold by piece, bunch, or packet step in whole numbers (1, 2, 3...).
2. When the customer opens the cart:
   * Prices re-verify against your latest prices in real-time.
   * If an item was just marked out of stock, the app warns the customer and blocks sending until removed.
3. The customer types their **Name**, **10-digit Phone Number**, and optional pickup time, then taps **"Send Order on WhatsApp"**.
4. WhatsApp opens with a pre-written order containing:
   * Customer Name & Phone
   * Neat line-by-line list of items, weights, and estimated prices
   * Estimated Total Amount
   * Pickup time note
5. You can reply directly on WhatsApp with: *"Order received! We are packing your fresh produce now."*

>
> 💡 **Discard Mistakes:** Made accidental changes to prices? Just tap **"Discard"** on the floating bottom bar before tapping Save.
